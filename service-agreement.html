Service agreement

This confirms the actual terms of your paid CrewSignal plan: what you're paying, how billing works, and how to cancel if you ever need to. It works alongside our existing Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and SMS Terms, which already cover consent responsibilities and don't need repeating here.

1. The parties

This agreement is between MylesHouse Technologies LLC, doing business as CrewSignal, a Tennessee limited liability company ("CrewSignal"), and the business named below ("Client").

2. Plan & pricing

Client is enrolling in the plan and rate indicated below. If Client is joining as a Founding Member, that rate is locked for the life of the account and will never increase, even as CrewSignal's standard pricing changes, as long as the account stays active and within its plan's size band.

A one-time setup fee applies unless waived below. Pricing is billed monthly in advance unless otherwise noted.

3. Term & billing

  • This is a month-to-month agreement. No long-term contract, no early-termination penalty.
  • Billing begins on the start date indicated below and recurs monthly on that same date.
  • If Client's team size grows or shrinks past their current plan's range, CrewSignal will contact Client before moving them to the correct tier. No client is moved to a higher tier without being told first.

4. Cancellation

  • Either party may cancel at any time by notifying the other in writing (email is sufficient).
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. CrewSignal does not provide partial-month refunds for a cycle already in progress.
  • Once cancelled, review-request sending stops at the end of that billing cycle. Client's historical send/click data is kept for 90 days afterward in case Client wants to reactivate, then deleted, unless Client asks for it to be deleted sooner or asks in writing for it to be kept longer.
  • Client can request a copy of their own send/click history at any time before it's deleted, at no charge.

5. Support

CrewSignal is a small, personal service, not a large support team, so response times reflect that directly. Normal support hours are Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm Central, by phone or email, with a response the same or next business day. Outside those hours, Client is welcome to reach out, and Brandon may well respond, but it isn't guaranteed, and shouldn't be relied on for anything urgent.

6. What Client is responsible for

Same standard already laid out in our Terms & Conditions and SMS Terms: Client collects their own customers' phone numbers as part of doing business with them, discloses at the point of service that a one-time review-request text will follow, and only marks a job complete when ready for that message to go out. CrewSignal does not guarantee any specific number of reviews, ratings, or business outcomes.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Do I have to change how I work?

No. You keep using the same software to run and close jobs. CrewSignal listens for the "job complete" signal from your CRM and handles the review request automatically. The only step you do is the one you already do.

Which software do you work with?

CrewSignal works with Square and QuickBooks Online today. Wave support is in active development (private beta). Don't see your software listed? Tell us what you use. Customer demand directly guides which integration we build next.

Is text-message review collection allowed?

Yes, when done correctly. CrewSignal uses a carrier-registered sending number, includes a clear opt-out ("Reply STOP") in every message, and processes opt-outs immediately. A customer can text START at any time to opt back in. You're responsible for letting your customers know at the time you collect their number that they'll get a review request text, which we cover in our Terms and SMS Terms.

What does the text actually say?

A short, friendly message with the customer's name, your business name, your Google review link, and an opt-out line. You can customize the wording so it sounds like you, not a corporate notification.

Can I control when the request sends?

Yes. CrewSignal can hold a completed job for a set delay before the request goes out, so it doesn't feel instant or robotic. We help you configure that timing around your normal customer experience.

Will my repeat customers get spammed every visit?

No. CrewSignal remembers when each customer was last contacted and applies a cooldown period before they're eligible for another request, so someone who comes in every week won't get hit every single time. Duplicate completion events are also automatically detected and ignored.

What does it cost to get started?

Pricing depends on how many people on your team are using CrewSignal, and we're currently offering a limited number of founding member rates locked in for life. Request a demo below and we'll walk you through the right plan for your business, no obligation.

How fast can I be up and running?

Most setups take a few days: connecting your CRM, confirming your Google review link, and getting your message wording dialed in. Once that's done, reviews start flowing after your very next completed job.

What if I don't have a Google Business Profile yet?

No problem, a lot of contractors don't. We'll walk you through setting one up in about 5 minutes as part of onboarding. See the setup guide here.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. Cancel anytime; details are in our Terms.
